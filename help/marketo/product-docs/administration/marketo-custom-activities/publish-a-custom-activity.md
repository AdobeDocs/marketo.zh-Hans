# 发布自定义活动 {#publish-a-custom-activity}

了解如何发布自定义活动。

1. 进入 **[!UICONTROL Admin]** 区域。

   ![](assets/publish-a-custom-activity-1.png)

1. 单击 **[!UICONTROL Marketo Custom Activities]**。

   ![](assets/publish-a-custom-activity-2.png)

1. 选择要发布的自定义活动。

   ![](assets/publish-a-custom-activity-3.png)

1. 点击 **[!UICONTROL Custom Activity Actions]** 下拉菜单，并选择 **[!UICONTROL Publish Activity]**。

   ![](assets/publish-a-custom-activity-4.png)

   自定义活动的[!UICONTROL State]更改自[!UICONTROL Draft]...

   ![](assets/publish-a-custom-activity-5.png)

   ...到[!UICONTROL Published]。

   ![](assets/publish-a-custom-activity-6.png)
